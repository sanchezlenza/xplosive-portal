About Us

Mission Statement

At Xplosive, dancers are pushed to grow, inspired to create, and empowered to shine. Our highly trained and experienced staff is committed to developing strong, confident, and versatile dancers while creating a positive, high-energy environment where every student feels supported.

Whether you're stepping into the studio for the first time or training at an elite level, Xplosive Dance Company is where talent is transformed into greatness. Join the movement. Feel the energy. Become part of the Xplosive family.

Our Story

Built by a Dance Family, For Dance Families

Ten years ago, a dream to open a dance studio grew into Xplosive Dance Company — built with careful planning, unwavering belief, and very humble beginnings. What started with a dream and a few dollars has grown into something truly special, built on passion, sacrifice, and heart.

Over the years, Xplosive has become known for success in the competitive dance world, earning numerous national titles and choreography awards — the result of dedicated dancers, coaches, and choreographers who continue to push boundaries and strive for excellence.

We take pride in being a small studio with an unstoppable spirit — a "small but mighty" team, true underdogs who consistently rise to compete among the very best. At Xplosive, we believe success is built on determination, work ethic, respect, and kindness.

— Ana Ricolt, Studio Director
